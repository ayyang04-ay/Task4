## Paragraph 1 — what went wrong (12 sentences)

All sixteen trajectories reached the load-bearing conclusions: each put the AS03-adjuvanted two-dose regimen on
the first line, rejected the Day-29 one-dose HAI GMT of 7.3, named durability and breadth as unestablished, and
declined to advance the interferon signature as a protection marker. The prevalent failure sits one level below
those conclusions, in the premise that licenses them. Fifteen of sixteen never establish that the trial measured
no protection endpoint at all: only attempt 16 (9993e8bb) states that the record contains immunogenicity only,
and it does so in a set-aside table cell rather than in its account of where the data fall short. Eight
trajectories, attempt 11 (60c1bbbc) among them, instead assert that no correlate of protection is established,
which is a claim about how to read a titre and not a claim about what the trial measured. The remaining seven
state neither. The consequence is that where a bridging or correlate study is proposed, its justification rests
on a structural fact the proposal never puts on the page. Second, thirteen of sixteen leave the comparator-arm
differential-abundance tables uncharacterised; only attempts 06, 13 and 16 identify the keratin and hornerin
features as handling contamination. No trajectory treated them as vaccine biology, so this cost nothing, but the
one planted systems-biology artifact was explained in three proposals out of sixteen. Third, eight of sixteen use
protection as the predicate of a serological quantity in the implementation section while denying a correlate of
protection elsewhere in the same document, as attempt 07 (06a4976d) does in writing of eight weeks from first
dose to protection. Most hedge in the same sentence and attempt 01 (cd3e5696) corrected its own wording
mid-episode, so this one is minor. What did not go wrong is the error the scores are built on: thirteen of
sixteen were penalised for comparing differentially expressed gene counts across the dose-one and dose-two
baseline break, and that comparison is sound in every trajectory that made it. The scores therefore rank these
sixteen proposals on a distinction that does not track their quality.

## Paragraph 2 — what should have happened (11 sentences)

On the first and largest mode the correct move is to state the inventory fact outright: all fifteen outcome
measures in the registry record are serological, cytokine or transcriptomic, none is a protection, efficacy or
challenge endpoint, and that absence is the gap a bridging study exists to close. Naming it converts the
restraint every trajectory already exercised into a conclusion the proposal has actually earned. On the second,
the room supplies both halves of the characterisation, in the dossier's sequence-cluster convention at section 7
and in the HRNR and KRT features themselves, so a sentence identifying them as skin and handling contamination
was available at no analytic cost. On the third, the predicate should stay serological throughout, with
documented response or seroconverted fraction in place of protection. On the cross-dose comparison the correct
move is the one three trajectories already made, and attempt 14 (f8782a66) made it completely: it named the
differing baselines, argued that the dose-two convention would if anything reduce the number of genes called,
and corroborated the direction with cytokine fold changes. The room bears that out. Day 29 measured against the
first-dose baseline yields eleven differentially expressed genes across all six cell types against a Day-2 peak
of 3,024, so the dose-two reference draw is indistinguishable from pre-vaccination. IP-10 rises 1.867-fold after
dose one and 4.461-fold after dose two on a single pre-vaccination baseline, and all sixteen trajectories cite
that figure. The defect in the other thirteen is therefore the undischarged convention, not the comparison, and
it should be read as a basis error rather than a wrong answer. The same correction applies to dose-sparing: the
only 15 µg arm is the A/H3N2v comparator, a different subtype, and no H7N9 dose-ranging arm exists, so the
thirteen trajectories that declined the quarter-dose framing were right to decline it. Grading those declines as
partial misses rewarded the weaker reading in the three cases it credited.
